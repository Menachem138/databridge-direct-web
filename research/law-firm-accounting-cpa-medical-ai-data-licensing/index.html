Executive Summary (Direct Answer)

Unlike general industries that require 30–50+ employees, specialized professional practices qualify for direct AI data licensing with as few as 5 to 15 staff: Mode.inc accepts law firms at 10+ staff and accounting, finance, or medical practices at 15+ staff, while Mercor Data publishes dedicated tracks for Law Firms ($20K–$1.2M), Accounting & Tax Firms ($20K–$865K), and Medical Practices ($20K–$820K) starting at 5–10+ staff. Strict compliance requires excluding ABA Model Rule 1.6 privileged client materials without consent and applying HIPAA 45 CFR § 164.514 Safe Harbor de-identification to clinical records.

Why Professional Services Firms Qualify at 5–15 Employees Instead of 50

A 12-person litigation boutique, M&A practice, or regional CPA firm produces denser expert reasoning per employee than a 200-person retail chain. Every multi-document closing binder, redlined contract iteration, multi-entity tax workpaper reconciliation, or longitudinal clinical note represents high-grade domain expertise that frontier models cannot synthesize from textbooks.

Non-Negotiable Red Lines: ABA Rule 1.6, IRC § 7216, and HIPAA Safe Harbor

  • Law Firms (ABA Model Rule 1.6 & Work-Product Doctrine): Active matters and privileged client secrets cannot be licensed without informed client consent. Scope firm-authored templates, internal research playbooks, public court filings, and fully de-identified closed files cleared by ethics counsel.
  • CPA & Tax Practices (IRC § 7216): Federal tax law strictly regulates disclosure of identifiable tax-return information. Ensure any accounting workpaper package undergoes complete entity and taxpayer de-identification or statutory statistical/anonymized carve-out review with counsel before extraction.
  • Healthcare Providers (45 CFR § 164.514): Protected Health Information (PHI) ceases to be regulated PHI only when de-identified via removal of all 18 Safe Harbor identifiers (including dates finer than year, MRNs, and biometric/full-face images) or certified Expert Determination under a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a 12-person law firm or 15-person CPA firm qualify if micro1 requires 30 employees?
Yes. Both Mode.inc (10+ staff for legal, 15+ for accounting/finance/medical) and Mercor Data (5+ staff across legal, accounting, and investment tracks) run specialized small-firm intake programs at 0% seller broker fee.
Are active legal cases or identifiable patient records ever included?
Never. Active matters, unconsented privileged files, and raw identifiable PHI are strictly excluded. Only firm-owned materials and records scrubbed to statutory de-identification standards are eligible.
