CISO & General Counsel Checklist: 9 Contract & VPC Anonymization Controls Before Licensing Corporate Data to AI

Published 2026-11-22 · Verified Primary Contract Audit · 8 min read · Author: DataBridge Advisory Research Desk
Executive Summary (Direct Answer)

Before signing an enterprise AI data license, CISOs and General Counsel should enforce nine mandatory controls: (1) direct principal contracting with zero middleman custody, (2) explicit opt-in system and folder scoping, (3) automated secret/credential/PII redaction prior to model delivery, (4) sample inspection rights before release, (5) written raw-data deletion schedules (e.g., 120-day deletion under micro1 Master Terms s.4.4) or in-VPC scrubbing (Scale AI), (6) two-party wiretap exclusion for unconsented call recordings, (7) GDPR/CCPA statutory anonymization verification, (8) allied-jurisdiction buyer restrictions, and (9) mutual limitation of liability.

The 9-Point Technical & Legal Gate for CISO / GC Sign-Off

  1. Principal Counterparty Verification: Confirm you are signing directly with a funded principal licensee (micro1 Inc., Mercor.io Corp., Mode.Inc, SourceX Inc., or Scale AI, Inc.) rather than an unfunded referral broker.
  2. Hard Exclusion Schedule in Writing: Carve out HR/payroll folders (ADP/Gusto/Workday), employee medical/accommodation notes, board M&A folders, active legal holds, and third-party NDA deliverables.
  3. Automated Secret & Credential Stripping: Require multi-pass scanning for AWS/GCP keys, OAuth tokens, database connection strings, SSNs/TINs, and bank routing numbers before staging.
  4. In-VPC Scrubbing When Egress Is Restricted: If corporate security policy forbids raw data leaving your cloud tenant, route the engagement to Scale AI Data Partnerships, where anonymization executes inside your own VPC/on-prem environment prior to package approval.
  5. Pre-Release Sample Audit: Retain contractual right to inspect de-identified output samples before downstream lab delivery.
  6. Two-Party Call Recording Filter: Strip or exclude customer/employee voice recordings involving two-party consent jurisdictions (e.g., California CIPA § 632, Florida, Pennsylvania, Illinois) unless explicit AI/analytics consent was captured at recording time.
  7. Raw Staging Deletion Certificate: Require written confirmation that raw ingested exports are purged on a fixed clock (such as 120 days) once de-identified derivatives pass QA.
  8. Permitted End-Buyers & Geographies: Lock in clauses restricting downstream licensing to vetted frontier labs in the US and allied jurisdictions.
  9. Liability Cap & Warranty Scope: Cap seller liability to fees actually received and narrow authority warranties strictly to company-owned operational records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can our security team anonymize data inside our own cloud before any AI buyer sees it?
Yes. Scale AI Data Partnerships specifically supports running de-identification pipelines inside your own AWS/GCP/Azure VPC or on-premise environment, with stakeholder approval gates on every outgoing package.
Why are recorded sales or support calls higher risk than ticket text?
Voice recordings carry biometric voiceprints and are governed by strict state wiretap statutes such as California's Invasion of Privacy Act (CIPA). Unless clear prior consent covers analytics/licensing, text transcripts scrubbed of PII or written support tickets are significantly safer to license.
