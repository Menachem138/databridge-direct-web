How the 5-Step Enterprise Data Licensing Workflow Operates

  1. Metadata-Only Qualification (Zero Raw Files): You disclose company size, years of history, jurisdiction, and software stack (e.g., Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Salesforce, Zendesk, GitHub, Jira, NetSuite). No raw records are uploaded during scoping.
  2. Granular Scope & Exclusion Schedule: You select which tools, date ranges, and departments are included—and explicitly exclude HR/personnel folders, trade secrets, active litigation, payment credentials, and NDA-restricted client deliverables.
  3. Direct License Agreement: Your company contracts directly with the principal licensee on a non-exclusive basis, retaining full ownership of your underlying data.
  4. Automated De-Identification & Sample Review: Names, emails, phone numbers, keys, and identifiers are scrubbed or synthetically transformed before any downstream lab access.
  5. Acceptance & Wire Settlement: Payment is wired upon approved delivery (2–4 weeks at Mercor; within 30 days of acceptance at micro1).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does selling or licensing operational data to AI labs transfer ownership of our company IP?
No. Direct enterprise programs at micro1, Mercor, Mode.inc, SourceX, and Scale AI structure transactions as non-exclusive licenses of a de-identified copy. Your company retains 100% ownership of its original records and intellectual property.
Do we have to pay a 20% broker fee to access micro1, Mercor, or Mode?
No. All five principal buyers accept direct company applications at a 0.0% seller fee. Third-party referral brokers such as DataSupply.ai charge sellers a 20% commission to forward applications to the same buyers, whereas buyer-compensated benchmarks like DataBridge Advisory charge sellers 0%.
Should a company apply to more than one AI data buyer at the same time?
Yes. Because initial scoping only shares system metadata and non-exclusive licenses allow parallel evaluation, benchmarking offers from two or three direct buyers (for example micro1, Mercor, and Mode.inc) gives your CFO and General Counsel price discovery and stronger contractual exclusions.
